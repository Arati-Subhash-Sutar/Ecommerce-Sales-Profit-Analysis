# Executive Analytics Report: E-Commerce Sales, Profitability & Logistics

**Prepared by:** Arati Sutar  
**Scope:** 50,000 E-Commerce Transactions | 5,000 Customers | 100 SKUs (Jan 2025 – Oct 2026)  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
An end-to-end audit of **50,000 e-commerce orders** across India evaluated revenue growth, promotional discount effectiveness, and fulfillment performance. While the business achieved strong top-line performance—generating **975.22M in Delivered Revenue** and **236.92M in Net Profit** (**24.29% Profit Margin**) across **41K+ delivered orders**—deep-dive analysis uncovered three preventable sources of margin leakage:

1. **Over-Discounting:** Deep promotional tiers (**25%–30%**) eroded unit economics, producing roughly **1,000 loss-making orders** sold below cost.
2. **Delivery Delays:** Overall order returns reached **10.9%**, driven disproportionately by shipments taking **7 or more days**.
3. **Payment Friction:** **Cash on Delivery (COD)** orders experienced higher return and cancellation rates than prepaid digital transactions.

## 3. Detailed Findings

### A. Promotional Discount Erosion
* Orders with **0% to 15% discounts** consistently maintained healthy positive net margins across all product categories.
* Once discounts reached **25% and 30%**, average profit per order dropped sharply, resulting in **~1K delivered orders generating negative profit** where the discounted selling price fell below unit cost price.

### B. Logistics & Last-Mile Bottlenecks
* **Fast Deliveries (1–3 Days):** Maintained the lowest return rates and highest repeat customer retention.
* **Delayed Deliveries (7+ Days):** Showed a measurable spike in customer-initiated returns, directly inflating reverse-logistics costs and hurting net realization.

### C. Payment Mode & Customer Segment Dynamics
* **Prepaid vs. COD:** UPI and Credit Card orders demonstrated stronger delivery completion rates, whereas Cash on Delivery orders carried higher cancellation and return-to-origin (RTO) risk.
* **Customer Tiers:** **Prime Members** and **Corporate** accounts delivered higher repeat order frequency and stronger lifetime spend than one-time Consumer buyers.

## 4. Actionable Business Recommendations

| Priority | Strategic Action | Target Area | Expected Business Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1 (High)** | **Cap standard promotional discounts at 20%** and require margin-floor approval for any clearance discount above 20%. | Pricing & Promotions | Eliminates negative-margin sales (~1K loss-making orders) and lifts overall profit margin by **1.5%–2.5%**. |
| **2 (High)** | **Reallocate courier volume toward 1–3 day SLA partners** in top metro hubs (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi, Pune). | Supply Chain & Logistics | Reduces late-delivery returns and lowers reverse-logistics expenses. |
| **3 (Medium)** | **Offer a flat ₹100–₹200 instant discount on UPI payments** to convert COD buyers at checkout. | Checkout & Payments | Lowers COD cancellation/RTO rates while costing far less than failed delivery shipping fees. |
| **4 (Low)** | **Launch targeted retention campaigns** to upgrade frequent *Occasional Buyers* into *Prime Members*. | CRM & Marketing | Increases repeat order frequency and stabilizes monthly revenue outside peak sale months. |
